Questions patients ask

Anything else, ask us on WhatsApp.

Do you still place amalgam fillings? +

No. The practice has been amalgam-free for about 30 years and uses metal-free materials: composite, glass ionomer and ceramics.

Should I have my amalgam fillings replaced? +

Fillings in good condition don't have to be replaced. We'll look at yours, explain what we see, and you decide. If you go ahead, removal follows protective steps.

We wait until after pregnancy and breastfeeding.

Do you offer metal-free implants? +

Yes. Zirconia (ceramic) implants as well as titanium, for a single tooth up to a full arch. Titanium has the longest track record; zirconia is a metal-free option with good results so far. We'll talk through which suits you.

What is Guided Biofilm Therapy? +

A thorough, gentle cleaning protocol. Warm water, air and a fine powder lift the biofilm from your teeth and gums, and ultrasonic instruments remove what's left. The practice is certified for it.

How do fees work? +

Fees depend on your treatment. Your written plan shows each level and its quote before you start, so you decide with the full picture. Which medical aids we work with, and how claims work, is to confirm.

I'm coming from outside Windhoek. Can I fit it into one trip? +

Tell us where you're travelling from when you request a time. Reception will try to group your visits into as few trips as possible.
