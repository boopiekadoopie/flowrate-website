Your first visit

What to expect, from the moment you request a time to the plan you take home.

Before

Getting ready

  • Reception confirms your time on WhatsApp
  • Bring your ID and medical aid card
  • Bring a list of medicines and supplements you take
  • Old X-rays, if you have them
On the day

About 45 to 60 minutes

  • A conversation about your health, not only your teeth
  • A full check of teeth, gums and bite
  • X-rays only if they're needed
  • Time for your questions
After

Your plan, in writing

  • Three levels: must be done, should be done, nice to have
  • A quote for each level before anything starts
  • You decide the pace
