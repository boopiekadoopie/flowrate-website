Dr Martin Wucher's path started in natural science, with microbiology and zoology, before dentistry and decades of clinical practice.

Growing up in Namibia, he saw how differently health developed in rural and urban communities over time. That contrast still shapes his work: health is created by the conditions people live in every day.

He doesn't diagnose, prescribe or replace your doctor. He helps families understand what's happening, recognise patterns early and make informed, confident decisions.
