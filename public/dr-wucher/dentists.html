Dr Martin WucherDentist since 1985
Dentist · biological dentistry and functional medicine

Dr Martin Wucher

Dr Wucher started in natural science, with microbiology and zoology at Rhodes, before qualifying as a dentist at the University of Pretoria in 1985.

Growing up in Namibia, he noticed how differently rural and urban communities stayed healthy. That question shaped his work: dentistry that looks at the whole person, alongside nutritional therapy and orthomolecular medicine.
