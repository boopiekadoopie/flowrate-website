# Free Website Breakdown: SOP

What to do when a "New lead: [name] (free breakdown request)" email arrives.
Target turnaround: within one business day (the site promises this).
Time budget per breakdown: 20 to 30 minutes.

## Step 1: Research (10-15 min)

Their website (skip if they have none):
- Open it on your phone first. That is how homeowners see it.
- Check: is there a phone number and a clear "get a quote" action visible
  without scrolling? Are there reviews or trust signals above the fold? Does
  it look current or dated?
- Run the URL through PageSpeed Insights (pagespeed.web.dev). Note the mobile
  score only.

Their Google presence (5 min, do this even if they have no website):
- Open an incognito window. Search "[their service] [their city]", e.g.
  "sprinkler repair Frisco TX".
- Note: are they in the Map Pack? On page one at all? Who holds the top 3
  spots?
- Open their Google Business Profile. Note review count and rating vs the top
  competitor, missing photos, missing services, last post date.

## Step 2: Write the breakdown (10 min)

Use the template below. Rules:
- Plain words. No jargon, no em-dashes, no emojis.
- Be honest, including about what they already do well.
- Every fix must be specific enough that they could act on it without us.
- Never quote a website build price. If they ask, it is scoped on the call.
- Do not trash their current provider or any competitor by name.

## Step 3: Send and log

- Send from andrew@flowrate.agency, reply to the address in the lead email.
- Subject: "Your free breakdown: [Business name]"
- Log the lead (date, name, city, sent date, response) in your lead tracker.
- If they reply or book, normal sales flow. If silence, one follow-up nudge
  after 4-5 days, then leave them alone (the form promises no spam).
